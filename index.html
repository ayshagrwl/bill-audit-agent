Google Sheets Setup Guide (3 Simple Steps)

Follow these instructions to connect your free Google Sheet in 2 minutes:

  1. Create a new Google Sheet at sheets.new (e.g. name it "Sales Agent Bill Custody").
  2. Click Extensions > Apps Script in the Google Sheet menu. Delete any code in the editor, and copy-paste the entire contents of google_apps_script.js (found in this app directory).
  3. Click the blue Deploy > New Deployment button:
    • Select type: Web App
    • Execute as: Me (your email)
    • Who has access: Anyone (necessary for your GitHub Pages app to send updates)
    • Click Deploy, grant permissions, and copy the Web app URL into the field above!
